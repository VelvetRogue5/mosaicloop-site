Mosaic Loop for iOS

This policy explains how Mosaic Loop handles information when you play. The current version does not require an account, does not connect to our servers, does not show ads, and does not use analytics, attribution, or tracking services. Your level progress and settings stay on your device.

Information Handled by the App

Mosaic Loop is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, precise or coarse location, photos, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, health data, or payment card details.

To provide the game, Mosaic Loop stores gameplay state and preferences on your device using the standard iOS user-defaults storage. This is limited to:

  • Progress: the level you have reached in the 1–30 level sequence.
  • Board state: the positions of the gems on the current board, the contents of the conveyor buffer, the level status, and the order in which colors were completed, so an unfinished level can be restored.
  • Settings: your music, sound effect, and vibration switches.
  • Tutorial state: whether the first-level tutorial has been completed.

None of this data identifies you, and none of it is transmitted off your device by the app. Mosaic Loop does not create an account identifier, a device fingerprint, or an advertising identifier.

No Network Data Collection

The current version of Mosaic Loop does not send gameplay data, device data, or diagnostic data to us or to any third party. There is no server-side account, no cloud save, no leaderboard, no remote configuration, and no in-app messaging.

Under Apple's App Store privacy questions, the current version is declared as Data Not Collected. If a future release adds a feature that transmits data, this policy and the App Store declaration will be updated before that release is submitted.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Mosaic Loop does not integrate third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution, or remote-configuration SDKs. It does not show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.

The game screen includes a continue button styled with a video icon. In the current version this button is interface only: it is not connected to any advertising network, it loads no ad, and it sends no request when tapped. If a future version connects it to a rewarded-ad provider, this policy will be updated first and the relevant SDK and its data practices will be disclosed here.

Mosaic Loop does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, does not read the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Required Reason APIs

The app bundle includes a privacy manifest declaring the Apple “required reason” APIs used by the game engine: user defaults (to read and write your local save), available disk space, file timestamps, and system uptime. These are used only to load and store local game data and to time animations. They are not used for fingerprinting, tracking, or advertising, and their values are not sent off the device.

Purchases

The build reviewed for this policy contains no in-app purchases and no purchase processing code. If a future version offers optional in-app purchases, they will be handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple would provide the app only with the product identifier, transaction status, and receipt needed to unlock the purchased item; we would not receive your payment card details. Apple handles that purchase and account data under the Apple Privacy Policy.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used only to provide game features: resume an unfinished level, unlock the next level, restore your audio and vibration preferences, and skip a tutorial you have already completed.

Information you voluntarily include in an email to us is used only to answer your request, troubleshoot the problem you report, and keep a record of the exchange.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player information, and we do not share it with advertising or analytics providers. Because the current version does not transmit data, there is nothing for us to share except information you choose to send us by email. We would disclose such information only when required by law or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data stays on your device until the app overwrites it during normal play or you delete the app and iOS removes its container. Mosaic Loop has no cloud save, so deleting the app permanently removes your progress and it cannot be restored by us.

If your device backs up to iCloud or to a computer, your save data may be included in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you through iOS settings and are governed by Apple's terms, not by us.

Emails you send us are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve the issue, and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete information you previously sent through email, subject to applicable law.

Security

The app keeps its data in the app's private storage area provided by iOS, and it transmits nothing. We use reasonable safeguards for the limited information we receive by email. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so please do not include sensitive personal information in a support message unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Children's Privacy

Mosaic Loop is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. It has no account creation, no chat or social features, no user-generated content, no behavioral advertising, and no gameplay analytics, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information a company holds about you. Because Mosaic Loop collects nothing, we normally hold no personal information about you at all; the only exception is the content of an email you choose to send us. Write to the address below and we will act on your request within the period required by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Mosaic Loop changes or when legal requirements change. The current version is always posted on this page and the “Last updated” date is revised. If a future release adds cloud saves, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, attribution, or any other data collection, this policy will be updated before that release ships.
