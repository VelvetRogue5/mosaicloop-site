Description

Every mosaic starts as a jumble of colored gems. Your job is to take it apart in the right order.

Mosaic Loop is a calm, handcrafted sorting puzzle. Tap a group of matching gems and they slide onto the loop that circles the board. Clear a full color and it leaves for good. Fill the loop with mismatched leftovers and you run out of room.

Thirty levels, each a different mosaic pattern, each solvable if you read the board before you tap. Pinch to zoom in on a crowded corner, pan to find the color you need, and watch the board refill as space opens up.

Features:
- 30 handcrafted mosaic levels
- One-tap gem sorting, no timers, no pressure
- A conveyor loop with a capacity meter that warns you before it fills
- Pinch-zoom and pan for larger boards
- A gentle tutorial on level one
- Restart a level instantly if you reach a dead end
- Music, sound, and vibration switches
- Plays entirely offline
- No account required
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- Progress stored on your device

Read the pattern, pick your order, and clear the loop.

App Store Review Notes Draft

  • Account required: No.
  • In-app purchases: None in the current build.
  • User-generated content: No.
  • Player communication: No.
  • Contains ads: No.
  • Tracking: No App Tracking Transparency prompt, no IDFA access, no cross-app tracking.
  • Data posture: Progress and preferences are stored locally; the app makes no network connection.
  • Note for reviewers: The continue button with a video icon in the dead-end dialog is interface only in this build and is not connected to any ad network.
  • Level pack: the iOS build ships the original abstract mosaic pack. The Android build ships a different, picture-based pack, so the two store listings must not share description copy.

App Privacy Declaration

Declare Data Not Collected. The app has no backend, no account, and no third-party SDK for advertising, analytics, attribution, crash reporting, or remote configuration. The privacy manifest declares four required-reason APIs (user defaults, disk space, file timestamp, system boot time), all used only for local game data and timing.
