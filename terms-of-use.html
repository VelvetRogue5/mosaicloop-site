4. Current Features

Mosaic Loop is a single-player puzzle game. The current version includes 30 levels, a first-level tutorial, grouped gem tapping, the conveyor loop with a capacity meter and overflow warning, board refills, a dead-end dialog that lets you restart the level or return to the level map, pinch-zoom and pan on the board from level two onward, completion and results animations, and music, sound, and vibration settings.

The current version does not include account login, player-to-player communication, user-generated content, cloud saves, in-app purchases, in-app advertising, behavioral tracking, or gameplay analytics. It does not connect to any server.
