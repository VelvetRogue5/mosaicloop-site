Full Description

Every mosaic starts as a jumble of colored gems. Your job is to take it apart in the right order.

Mosaic Loop is a calm, handcrafted sorting puzzle. Tap a group of matching gems and they slide onto the loop that circles the board. Clear a full color and it leaves for good. Fill the loop with mismatched leftovers and you run out of room.

Thirty levels, each a different mosaic pattern, each solvable if you read the board before you tap. Pinch to zoom in on a crowded corner, pan to find the color you need, and watch the board refill as space opens up.

Features:
- 30 handcrafted mosaic levels
- One-tap gem sorting, no timers, no pressure
- A conveyor loop with a capacity meter that warns you before it fills
- Pinch-zoom and pan for larger boards
- A gentle tutorial on level one
- Restart a level instantly if you reach a dead end
- Music, sound, and vibration switches
- Plays entirely offline: the app has no internet permission
- No account required
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- Progress stored on your device

Read the pattern, pick your order, and clear the loop.

Data Safety Draft

Declare no data collected and no data shared. The app bundles no analytics, advertising, attribution, purchase, or tracking library, does not access the Android Advertising ID, and does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot transmit anything. Level progress and settings stay in the app's private storage.

Answer yes to “users can request data deletion” by pointing to the local option: clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes everything. Note that the app allows Android Backup, so the local save may appear in the user's own Google account backup. Confirm every Data safety answer against the exact production app bundle before submission.
