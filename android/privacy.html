Mosaic Loop for Android

This policy explains how Mosaic Loop handles information when you play on Android. The app requests no internet permission, requires no account, shows no ads, and uses no analytics, attribution, or tracking services. Your level progress and settings stay on your device.

Information Handled by the App

Mosaic Loop is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, photos, files, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, or payment details.

To provide the game, Mosaic Loop stores gameplay state and preferences on your device using Android's private shared-preferences storage for this app. This is limited to:

  • Progress: the level you have reached in the 1–30 level sequence.
  • Board state: the positions of the gems on the current board, the contents of the conveyor buffer, the level status, and the order in which colors were completed, so an unfinished level can be restored.
  • Settings: your music, sound effect, and vibration switches.
  • Tutorial state: whether the first-level tutorial has been completed.

None of this data identifies you, and none of it is transmitted off your device by the app. Mosaic Loop does not create an account identifier, a device fingerprint, or an advertising identifier.

Permissions

The app's manifest declares one permission, android.permission.VIBRATE, which produces short haptic ticks when you move a gem. It is a normal permission, it shows no runtime prompt, and you can turn the effect off with the Vibrate switch in the in-game settings panel.

Mosaic Loop does not declare android.permission.INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. Without the internet permission the operating system prevents the app from opening a network connection at all, so it cannot send your data anywhere, even accidentally. It also declares no location, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, phone-state, or advertising-ID permission.

No Network Data Collection

The current version of Mosaic Loop does not send gameplay data, device data, or diagnostic data to us or to any third party. There is no server-side account, no cloud save, no leaderboard, no remote configuration, and no in-app messaging.

In the Google Play Data safety section, the current version is declared as collecting no data and sharing no data. If a future release adds a feature that transmits data, this policy and the Data safety declaration will be updated before that release is published.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Mosaic Loop bundles no third-party libraries for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, attribution, or remote configuration. It shows no banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.

The game screen includes a continue button styled with a video icon. In the current version this button is interface only: it is not connected to any advertising network, it loads no ad, and it sends no request when tapped. If a future version connects it to a rewarded-ad provider, this policy will be updated first and the relevant SDK and its data practices will be disclosed here.

The app does not read the Google Advertising ID, does not build advertising profiles, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Android Backup

The app allows Android's standard backup service, so your local save file may be included in the device backup that Android makes to your own Google account and restored when you set up a new device. That backup belongs to you and is controlled from your device and Google account settings; we have no access to it. It is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off in your device settings, or clear the app's data to remove the local save.

Purchases

The build reviewed for this policy contains no in-app purchases and does not integrate Google Play Billing. If a future version offers optional in-app purchases, they will be processed by Google Play. Google would provide the app only with the product identifier, purchase state, and purchase token needed to unlock the purchased item; we would not receive your payment card details.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used only to provide game features: resume an unfinished level, unlock the next level, restore your audio and vibration preferences, and skip a tutorial you have already completed.

Information you voluntarily include in an email to us is used only to answer your request, troubleshoot the problem you report, and keep a record of the exchange.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player information, and we do not share it with advertising or analytics providers. Because the app transmits nothing, there is nothing for us to share except information you choose to send us by email. We would disclose such information only when required by law or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data stays on your device until the app overwrites it during normal play, you clear the app's data or storage, or you uninstall the app. Mosaic Loop has no cloud save, so removing local data permanently removes your progress and it cannot be restored by us. A copy may persist in your own Android backup until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Emails you send us are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve the issue, and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete information you previously sent through email, subject to applicable law.

Security

The app keeps its data in the private app directory that Android isolates from other apps, and it transmits nothing. We use reasonable safeguards for the limited information we receive by email. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so please do not include sensitive personal information in a support message unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Children's Privacy

Mosaic Loop is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. It has no account creation, no chat or social features, no user-generated content, no behavioral advertising, and no gameplay analytics, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information a company holds about you. Because Mosaic Loop collects nothing, we normally hold no personal information about you at all; the only exception is the content of an email you choose to send us. Write to the address below and we will act on your request within the period required by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Mosaic Loop changes or when legal requirements change. The current version is always posted on this page and the “Last updated” date is revised. If a future release adds cloud saves, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, attribution, or any other data collection, this policy will be updated before that release ships.
