What to Include

Please include your app version, device model, operating-system version, the level number, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot when the issue involves the board layout, the conveyor loop, an animation, or a crash. We reply from the address above; nothing is collected from the app itself, so the details you send are all we have to work with.

Common Gameplay Questions

The loop filled up and I could not continue: the capacity meter above the loop shows how much room is left and warns you when it is nearly full. Gems only leave the loop when a whole color is complete, so collect one color at a time instead of tapping every group you see. If you reach a dead end, the dialog lets you restart the level or return to the level map.

I cannot see the gems on a large board: from level two onward you can pinch to zoom and drag to pan the board.

My progress disappeared: progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app on iOS, or clearing app data on Android, removes it permanently. There is no cloud save, so we cannot restore it.

Sound or vibration is missing: check the music, sound, and vibration switches in the in-game settings panel, then check your device volume, silent mode, and system vibration settings.

What is the button with the video icon? In the current version it is interface only. It is not connected to any ad network and loads nothing.

Purchases and Refunds

The current build contains no in-app purchases on either platform, so there is nothing to buy and nothing to refund. If purchases are offered in a future version, they will be processed by the store you downloaded the game from, and billing questions should go to Apple's purchase support or Google Play refund help. Never send us full payment-card details.

Privacy Policies

Mosaic Loop stores progress locally and includes no advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking SDK. Read the iOS Privacy Policy or the Android Privacy Policy.
